Ashebir Diriba

Ashebir Diriba is a software developer focused on building products from idea to implementation. He works across web applications, desktop tools, and open-source libraries, with an emphasis on turning concepts into functional systems that solve real problems.

Previously, Ashebir developed internal tooling and production applications, including platforms for food ordering and table reservation, as well as desktop utilities that automate spreadsheet workflows. He is also the creator of ShieldStack, a security middleware package for Express.js, and continues to explore the intersection of animation and artificial intelligence through projects such as Manai.

Before entering technology, Ashebir was a self-taught realistic sketch artist. The discipline, precision, and problem-solving habits developed through that practice continue to shape his approach to software engineering.
